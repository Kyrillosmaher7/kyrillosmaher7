<b> 🧬 About Me </b>

<div align="center">

I am a **Computer Science graduate student at Ain Shams University (ASU), Cairo**, focused on **Artificial Intelligence research**, with a specialization in:

- 🎙️ Speech Recognition (ASR)
- 🧠 Deep Learning & Neural Networks
- 📚 Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- 🔊 Speech & Audio Intelligence Systems

My research interest lies in **bridging human speech and machine understanding**, especially in **assistive technologies for speech disorders such as stuttering (SPL systems)**.

I aim to build AI systems that move beyond accuracy — toward **real human impact in communication and healthcare**.

---

## 🎯 Research Interests

- Speech-to-Text & End-to-End ASR Systems  
- Transformer-based NLP architectures (BERT, Whisper-like models)  
- Self-supervised learning for speech representations  
- Audio feature learning (MFCC, spectrogram-based modeling)  
- AI for healthcare & speech therapy assistance  
- Human-centered AI for communication disorders
